Beyond Bot

A .NET console application that automates trading on Bybit.

  • .NET
  • Bybit
  • Trading
GitHub · [repository link]

What it does

  • Connects to Bybit through a dedicated controller layer and works with market and trading endpoints.
  • Manages symbols, timeframes, market data, indicators and orders from a console menu.
  • Runs automated strategies, which are separate modules that can be swapped or changed without rewriting the app.
  • Calculates the EMA, RMA, ATR and Adaptive Envelope indicators, caching the values for repeated evaluations.
  • Stores bot data in MySQL.
  • Publishes as a self-contained Linux build and runs continuously as a service.

Best suited to developers and technical traders who want a customisable, code-first foundation rather than a no-code panel.
