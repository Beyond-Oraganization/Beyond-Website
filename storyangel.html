The Angel Beyond

Story

How a man became an angel.

The story began when I woke up in the morning. Someone was knocking on the window. I decided to see who it was. I walked up to the window and pushed the curtain aside. There was an angel outside. I was frightened, but he calmed me down. He introduced himself as Beyond. He decided to tell me his story: the story of how he became an angel.

Once there lived Beyond. As he climbed the mountain toward heaven, he was expecting paradise. But when Beyond arrived, he saw hell: people were unhappy, there was a heap of senseless pain, suffering, lust, envy and anger. Beyond went in, because he had nowhere else to go. Like the others, he began to suffer, but he did not complain. He accepted that this is how it is and how it has to be. Without hatred, simply with understanding. Suffering became his peace. The world slowly pushed him deeper into the grave. He did not understand why he should go on living. And then he looked around and saw that the others were suffering too. So Beyond decided that even if he suffers, he can still stop such suffering for others.

For this he was taken up to heaven. He thought they would never accept him there again, and he had resigned himself. But he was warmly received. He was given a spear and a shield. He was led to the edge of heaven and shown how dark and terrifying it is below, compared to the heights. He was told that he came from there, and that his mission was to return and restore order. Beyond was very frightened and did not want to go. Going there was like voluntarily plunging into cold water. Yet he decided to descend. That is how Beyond became an angel.
