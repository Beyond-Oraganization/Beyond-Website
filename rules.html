Ideology

The rules.

The rules are meant to contradict one another on purpose: that is how they are refined.

Contents
  1. Rules 01–09
  2. Rules 10–18
  3. Rules 19–27

Rules 01–09

The first nine
  1. The poor man is not the one who has nothing, but the one who desires.
  2. In people, the abstract matters more than the real.
  3. Ideas that people are willing to pay for are often small and quiet.
  4. He who has a long time horizon receives; the others lose.
  5. Punish yourself for what you can influence; there is no point in punishing yourself for the rest.
  6. Money was created for exchange, but it is also wealth. So it is easiest to get it where exchange is greatest, and then you only have to keep it.
  7. I may be a rake, but I am noble and lofty.
  8. Being in calm and predictability is better than being in stress and spontaneity.
  9. The 20% rule gives 80% of the results. So we should spend 80% of our strength in four directions to get the maximum results.

Rules 10–18

The next nine
  1. Life always asks for more than you give.
  2. Strike with lightness in the heart and ruthlessness in action.
  3. Knowledge is a sin, and it should be obtained like one: by accident.
  4. Five steps of enlightenment: the presence of limits, the absence of a goal, the absence of subjectivity, the absence of knowledge, the absence of company.
  5. Listen about good when it is not needed; listen about evil when it is not needed.
  6. Memory is important for victory.
  7. If desire is a pure manifestation of the devil, then actions against it are a war with it.
  8. Private efforts must be privately useful.
  9. If your life is a house, then both the foundation and the final effect must be strong and preserved.

Rules 19–27

The last nine
  1. The sacrifice of truth saves.
  2. The place of a good woman is the same as that of the Senate in Rome; likewise that of a man is the dictator’s.
  3. Good attention is the kind that is a caress to the other person.
  4. If you must put on a mask to achieve something, then it is something you do not need.
  5. The real test is victory.
  6. When you look for reasons to do something, do not. When you look for reasons not to do something, do it.
  7. Ask people what their problem is and solve it so that you come out ahead.
  8. You have to wake up the way you want to work throughout the day.
  9. For your wealth to be inherited, you must pass on not potential but realisation; otherwise it is unlikely that anyone will want to spend their life on your unrealised potential.
