Ants Must Not Bite an Elephant

Story

What happens to those who dig under a giant.

My friends Alina and Vita were looking for work. They were not picky about vacancies, because for a long time nothing had worked out for them. After a long search, through connections, they found a job at one huge firm, as cleaners.

The brutal building of the corporation that they were to clean was monstrously huge. It was so big that its dimensions were comparable to a metropolis. It was a small city made of two disproportionately joined parallelepipeds: a small lower one stood like a book on a shelf under the elongated, larger one in front, which rested on the smaller one as if on a column supporting its rear part. The large windows did not help reduce its size, but only increased it, because of how many of them there were on the walls. If you grasp the size of those continuous windows (they were about two or three people tall), you feel uneasy seeing how ordinary they were. The entrance to the lower, smaller section was lit with a white light that did not even reach the upper block. Slightly sinister in its size, the building stands in the middle of a field surrounded by forest, which separated the company’s territory from the city.

After getting the job, Alina did not get in touch for a long time. Sensing a quiet danger that, like a predator, hid in the shadow of calm, I decided to ask people more about the company.

I found out that the building is generally divided into three parts: residential, the company’s offices, and a third, unclear one, usually called the construction part. There were also stories about people disappearing. People said the work was good, but from time to time strange things happened: harassment, bribery, payment for silence or blackmail. So I decided to pull Alina and Vita out of this swamp and started looking for more information about the company, but found only more information about the division of the building: in fact it was not only the “construction” part that was unclear, but also the office part, where most of the incidents took place.

After some time I found out that Vita had quit that job. This only confirmed my fears about the company. I talked to her, but she gave me no concrete information, so I could draw conclusions only from the very fact that she quit. The more I learned about the company, the more I understood that digging under it was dangerous.

After a few more days of searching I am sitting in a park on a bench. The day was bright. Two stylishly and neatly dressed people came up to me. The two men were of different heights. The taller one inspired an inner unease. I stand up, leaving my things on the bench. His movements and low timbre of voice killed any hope of negotiation (he was the first to approach):

– Good day. I have come to warn you against any further actions or investigations concerning our company. Stop this.

I feel naked, my thoughts as if fully written out on an abstract sheet that is read without any cover. Understanding that the situation is lost, I want to beg for at least some part of freedom. Collecting myself, I decide to throw at the man, who was already walking back, my veiled ultimatum:

– I do not understand why you are so aggressive toward me! What exactly can’t I do? Even google you?!

They answered me harshly and clearly. The taller man, like a stately warrior with a spear in his hand, finishes off all my still-living impulses. He answers me with a hint of a sigh, but harshly and clearly:

– You have no right to speak to me in a raised tone. You have forgotten your position. You will pay for it.

Calmly coming up to me, he hit me. Losing consciousness, I noticed the shorter one rummaging through my things.

After I came to, I immediately checked my things. Everything was in place, but the phone would not turn on even after charging, and the computer, once turned on, showed a single screen that could not be changed in any way. Two or three days later I was taken to a psychiatric ward with a diagnosis of paranoia.
