Conclusions

What the treatise has concluded so far.

The treatise is a more logical text than the myths. It is written slowly, over many years, and these are the conclusions it has reached to date.

Read the full treatise

  • A ladder of three steps

    The starting state, the goal and the method.

  • Three roles

    Science is the method, philosophy is the goal, and religion binds actions to emotion. Religion exists not for the sake of truth, but so that people actually follow the rules that reason devised.

  • Faith is a selected tradition

    It survived a selection that no single life could reproduce.

  • Knowledge costs you your convictions

    To know, you must rebel against your own values.

  • God is connection

    Including the connection between cause and effect.

  • Contradictions are on purpose

    Like a blacksmith forging a blade, they refine the rules and keep one-sided views and rubbish out.
