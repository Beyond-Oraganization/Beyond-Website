Ideology

People are close because of the ideas they live by.

Beyondia is an idea that holds that people are made close by the ideas they live by.

In practice it is a kind of pseudo-country, organisation and identity — a nation defined by what its people believe, not by territory. It stands for constant expansion, for the right to say things as they are, for judging by results over the long run, and for continuous growth: learning, and putting what you know to use.
