Ideology

Four principles.

What a Beyondian lives by, in the order it is usually learned.

  • Constant expansion

    Always growing — in knowledge, in craft, in reach. Beyondia stands for permanent expansion, never for standing still.

  • The right to plain speech

    To say things as they are, without hiding behind a lie. The strength of a Beyondian is the ability to speak openly and exactly.

  • A long horizon

    Judge by results over the longest view: the further, the better. Everyone may think the Beyondian is losing, but he keeps working. He knows where real strength is, and in the end he wins, like in a marathon.

  • Knowledge in use

    Continuous development: to learn without pause, and to put what you know to work.
